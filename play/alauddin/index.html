Alauddin — play free online

Alauddin and the Brass Lamp: an Arabian Nights rooftop adventure in 10 levels.

On a computer, click the game, then use the keyboard. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

How to play Alauddin

  • ← → or A D — run
  • Space / Z — jump (press again mid-air to double jump, or at a wall to wall-jump)
  • J / X — sword, K / C — throw an apple
  • L / Shift — roll under blades, hold ↓ on slopes to slide
  • ↑ ↓ — climb ropes, E — Nazim's djinn blast, P — pause

100 gems or every golden scarab in a level earns an extra life. Smash pots for apples, hearts and gifts. Read how to beat Alauddin for more tips.
