Max Rampage — play free online

Suburban Stunt Legend: flip, spin and grab your way through 9 episodes, then beat Chad Stuntly.

Turn your phone sideways for the best view.

On a computer, click the game, then use the keyboard. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

How to play Max Rampage

  • → speed up, ← slow down
  • Hold Space to charge, release at the ramp lip for massive air
  • In the air: Z spin, X flip, hold C grab (add ↑ / ↓ for variations)
  • ↑ ↓ in the air to level out for a PERFECT landing
  • P pause, M mute. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

Chain several tricks in one jump for a combo multiplier, and fill the trick meter to trigger MEGA STUNT mode. Collect coins to buy helmets, suits, rides and trails in the Stunt Garage. More in our Max Rampage tips guide.
