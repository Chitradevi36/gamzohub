Max Rampage Tips: Land Every Stunt and Beat Chad

Nov 10, 2026

Max Rampage looks chaotic, but every big score comes from the same few habits. Here's how to go from wipeout city to Suburban Stunt Legend.

1. Charge, then release at the lip

Hold jump to charge while you roll toward a ramp, and let go right as you reach the top edge. Releasing at the lip combines your charge with the ramp's launch for massive air. Releasing early wastes most of it.

2. Level out before you land

Tricks score nothing if you crash. In the air, use up and down to tilt Max so the board matches the ground. A flat landing earns a PERFECT bonus (1.5x); a crooked one is SKETCHY (0.6x) or a crash.

3. Chain tricks in one jump

Your combo is multiplied by the number of tricks in it. A spin plus a flip plus a grab in one jump is worth far more than three single tricks. Start tricks early in the jump so they finish before you land, because an unfinished trick is a crash.

4. Know your trick variations

Spin (Z) becomes a Helicopter 720 with up held, or a Quick Spin with down. Flip (X) becomes a Double Flip with up, or a Backflip with left. Hold grab (C) longer for more points; up and down change the grab type.

5. Fill the trick meter for MEGA STUNT

Every landed combo fills the meter at the top of the screen. When it's full you get five seconds of MEGA STUNT: triple points, guaranteed landings and smashing through hazards. Save your best combos for that window.

6. Earn all three stars

One star for finishing, one for beating the episode's score goal, and one for grabbing the golden wrench and landing a three-trick combo in the same run. The wrench floats high above a big ramp roughly halfway through the level, so charge fully before that jump.

7. Beating Chad's Mega Ramp Rally

In the final episode Chad calls out a trick. Land that exact trick before his countdown runs out to earn 800 points and knock him back; miss it and he scores instead. Keep your speed up, land clean, and save MEGA STUNT for the last stretch before the finish.
