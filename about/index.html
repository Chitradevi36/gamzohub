About GamzoHub

A small, focused library of browser games you can trust to be fast, free and fair.

What we're building

GamzoHub is a home for polished, replayable browser games. Our first two titles are Max Rampage, a comic suburban stunt game, and Alauddin, an Arabian Nights platformer. We grow the library one finished game at a time rather than filling it with low-effort clones.

Our approach

Every game on GamzoHub runs instantly in your browser — no install, no account required to play. We keep the free tier genuinely good: new games launch free, and any future paid features are designed to add convenience on top, never to lock casual players out.

Who's behind it

GamzoHub is an independent project built and maintained by a small team. Strategy videos and new-release trailers are on the way; until then, the fastest way to reach us is the contact page.
